# Google Business Profile: What Is It and How to List Your Business?

**Google Business Profile** is the free online listing that helps a business appear in Google search results and Google Maps. It allows customers to discover the business, view its location, contact details, services, photos, reviews, and working hours.

Google Business Profile is now commonly called **Google Business Profile**, while the older name **Google My Business** is still familiar to many users.

---

## Why Use Google Business Profile?

A Google Business Profile helps a business:

- Appear in local search results
- Appear on Google Maps
- Show its address, phone number, and website
- Display opening hours
- Receive customer reviews
- Share photos and updates
- Receive customer questions
- Present services and categories
- Improve local visibility

A business profile is especially important for businesses that serve customers in a particular area, such as restaurants, shops, clinics, offices, and service providers.

---

## Requirements for Creating a Profile

Before creating a profile, the business should have:

- A verified business name
- A physical location or service area
- A phone number
- A website or social media page
- A business address, if customers are expected to visit
- A business category
- A verified email address or Google account

A business that operates only online may still create a profile if it has a service area and customers can contact it.

---

## How to List Your Business

### Step 1: Sign in to Google Business Profile

1. Sign in to a Google account.
2. Open the Google Business Profile website.
3. Select **Manage your business**.
4. Choose **Add your business** or **Create a profile**.

### Step 2: Enter Your Business Name

Enter the exact business name that customers recognize. Use the official business name and avoid adding unnecessary information.

### Step 3: Choose a Business Category

Select the main category that best describes the business.

Examples include:

- Restaurant
- Clinic
- Retail store
- Design agency
- Software company
- Education center
- Repair service

A business may add additional categories later.

### Step 4: Add Location Information

If the business has a physical location, enter:

- Street address
- City
- State or province
- Postal or ZIP code
- Country

If the business serves a delivery or service area, choose the relevant service area and avoid publishing an incorrect address.

### Step 5: Add Contact Information

Enter the business phone number and website address. If the business does not have a website, it may leave the field blank or add a social media link.

### Step 6: Add Service Areas

If the business serves customers outside its physical location, add the relevant service areas. This is useful for businesses such as tutors, cleaners, consultants, and delivery services.

### Step 7: Choose Business Features

Select the features that apply, such as:

- Health and wellness
- Food and drink
- Professional services
- Local services
- Retail
- Education
- Transportation
- Entertainment

### Step 8: Add Business Details

Complete the following information when available:

- Business description
- Opening hours
- Services
- Products
- Images
- Website
- Phone number
- Email address
- Social media links

### Step 9: Save the Profile

Click **Continue** or **Create profile** after completing the required information.

### Step 10: Verify the Business

Google may ask the business owner to verify the business location through:

- A verification code sent by email or phone
- A video confirmation
- A postcard sent to the business address
- An email or phone verification process

The verification step is necessary because it proves that the business is real and located at the stated address.

## Google Business Profile Verification

Verification may include the following methods:

### Postal Verification

Google sends a postcard containing a verification code to the business address. The owner enters the code into the profile.

### Video Verification

Google may request a video showing the business location, signage, interior, and equipment.

### Phone or Email Verification

The business owner may receive a code by phone or email. The code is entered into the profile to prove ownership.

### Google Business Profile Manager

An owner can add managers who have permission to manage the profile. Managers should use a verified Google account.

> We provide professional software development services, website design, and mobile application development for small businesses.

Enter the days and times when the business is open. If the business is closed on some days, set the correct hours or mark the day as closed.

Add clear photos of:

### Add Services and Products

Include the main services, products, locations, or special offerings. Avoid adding information that is inaccurate or misleading.

### Add Reviews

Ask satisfied customers to leave honest reviews. Respond to all reviews professionally and respectfully.
